Frequently Asked Questions

Why does Securamail require Google OAuth authorization?
Securamail uses Google OAuth 2.0 to access your email messages directly through the official Gmail API. This allows our analysis engines to inspect message headers, hyperlinks, and attachments for phishing threats without ever needing or storing your Google account password.
Does Securamail modify or delete my emails in Gmail?
No. Securamail is designed for security inspection and threat triage. We do not modify, delete, or alter your messages inside your actual Google Workspace or Gmail mailbox.
Does Securamail sell my data or train public AI models?
Never. Securamail strictly complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy. We do not sell personal data or email contents to any third party, nor do we use Gmail data to train generalized AI models.
How do I disconnect my Gmail account?
You can disconnect your account at any time from your Securamail Settings or Profile page. Alternatively, you can revoke access at any time through Google Account Security Settings at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
How does the threat detection score work?
Securamail evaluates emails on a 0–100 risk scale. The engine checks sender authentication (SPF/DKIM/DMARC), compares hyperlinks against Google Safe Browsing and VirusTotal reputation databases, inspects attachments using our Attachment Threat Analysis Engine (ATAE), and analyzes message text for deceptive phrasing.
