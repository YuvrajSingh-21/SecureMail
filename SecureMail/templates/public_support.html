Frequently Asked Questions

Why did my Google OAuth connection fail?
Ensure that you are granting the requested permissions. SecureMail requires read access to evaluate your inbox. If you deny the scope, authentication will fail. Try disconnecting and reconnecting from the dashboard.
Does SecureMail delete my emails?
No. SecureMail is currently configured as a read-only analysis tool. We tag and flag threats within our own dashboard, but we do not alter or delete emails in your actual Google Workspace or Gmail account.
I received an email but it's not showing up.
Our synchronization engine pulls new emails periodically. There may be a short delay (up to 5 minutes) before new emails appear in the SecureMail dashboard. You can click the "Refresh" button in your Inbox view to force an immediate sync.
