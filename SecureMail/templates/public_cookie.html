Cookie Policy

We respect your privacy. SecuraMail uses zero tracking, marketing, or analytics cookies.

Essential Cookies

We only deploy cookies that are strictly necessary to run the application securely. You cannot opt out of these if you wish to use SecuraMail.

Authentication (Auth)

Maintains your authenticated state after you log in via Google OAuth. This ensures you do not have to log in repeatedly on every page.

Cross-Site Request Forgery (CSRF)

A crucial security measure that prevents malicious sites from making unauthorized requests on your behalf while you are logged in to SecuraMail.

Session Data

Temporarily stores required application state as you navigate between pages. This cookie expires when you close your browser or log out.
