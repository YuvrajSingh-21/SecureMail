About the Project

About SecureMail

SecureMail is a cybersecurity-focused email security platform that helps users securely connect their Gmail account, analyze email content for phishing and malicious threats, and identify potentially dangerous emails and attachments.

Our Mission

To provide email users with transparent, understandable cybersecurity tooling that proactively highlights deceptive senders, phishing attempts, malicious links, and dangerous attachments without compromising privacy.

Transparent Detection

Rather than operating as an opaque black box, SecureMail provides clear, human-readable breakdowns explaining why an email was scored as risky, including specific indicator highlights.

Multi-Layer Threat Architecture

1

Google OAuth Connection

Users connect their Gmail account via official Google OAuth 2.0. Access tokens and refresh tokens are encrypted at rest using AES-256 encryption.

2

Multi-Factor Threat Scanning

Messages are evaluated for SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment, suspicious keywords, URL reputations (Google Safe Browsing & VirusTotal), and attachment anomalies (ATAE).

3

Actionable Insights

Each email receives a 0–100 risk score and concise AI-assisted diagnostic summaries to help users quickly assess and respond to incoming threats.

Core Security Pillars

Encrypted Credentials

All Google OAuth tokens are encrypted using AES-256 at rest and transmitted strictly over TLS 1.2+.

Privacy-First Policy

We never sell personal data, never share email content, and adhere to the Google API User Data Policy.

User Ownership

You can disconnect your Gmail account at any time with one click, revoking all credentials immediately.

Development & Operational Information
