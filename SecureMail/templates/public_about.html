About SecuraMail

Protecting your digital life through intelligent, explainable email security.

Our Mission

To create a transparent, intelligent communication environment where users never have to second-guess the safety of an email.

Our Vision

A future where phishing and deceptive tactics are obsolete, blocked seamlessly by on-device ML that respects user privacy.

Why & How We Do It

The "Why"

Email remains the primary attack vector for cyber threats. Legacy filters are black boxes that fail to adapt. We built SecuraMail to empower users with human-readable threat analysis.

The "How"

By combining Local Machine Learning, global Threat Intelligence (like VirusTotal and Google Safe Browsing), and deep Gmail integration, we analyze emails in real-time without compromising privacy.

Core Pillars

Privacy First

Your data never leaves your control. No reading, no selling.

Local ML

Advanced language models run locally to catch deceptive intents.

Google Integration

Seamless OAuth connection with Gmail for effortless synchronization.

Product Roadmap

Phase 1: Foundation

Phase 2: Intelligence

Phase 3: Ecosystem

Browser extensions, custom enterprise rules, cross-provider support.
