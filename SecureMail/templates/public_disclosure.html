Responsible Disclosure

Security is a collaborative effort. We welcome and reward the security community for identifying vulnerabilities in our platform.

In Scope

We are primarily interested in vulnerabilities that could compromise the integrity of our platform or user data:

  • Authentication & Authorization bypass
  • Cross-Site Scripting (XSS)
  • Cross-Site Request Forgery (CSRF)
  • Server-Side Request Forgery (SSRF)
  • SQL Injection
  • Data exposures (e.g. bypassing ML sanitization)

Out of Scope

Please refrain from testing the following:

  • Volumetric attacks (DDoS/DoS)
  • Social engineering or phishing against SecureMail staff
  • Physical security of our offices
  • Third-party services (Google OAuth, VirusTotal, etc.)
  • Lack of standard security headers not leading to a direct vulnerability

How to Report

Please submit a detailed report containing proof-of-concept steps to reproduce the vulnerability.

Safe Harbor

If you conduct your research in good faith and adhere to this policy, we will not initiate legal action against you or ask law enforcement to investigate you.
