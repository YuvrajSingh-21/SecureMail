Privacy Policy

Securamail is committed to protecting your privacy and securing your email data. This policy explains how we collect, process, protect, and handle your information when you use https://securamail.me/.

1. Overview & Application Purpose

Securamail ("we", "us", "our") is an AI-powered email security application accessible at https://securamail.me/. The platform helps users identify email threats, phishing attempts, suspicious hyperlinks, and dangerous attachments in their connected Gmail accounts.

The platform is maintained and operated by the Asteroids development team. For any privacy or security inquiries, please contact us at team.asteroids.2024@gmail.com.

2. Information We Access and Process

To provide automated security analysis, Securamail accesses the following categories of data with your explicit authorization:

  • Google Account Information: Your primary email address, full name, and avatar URL provided through Google OAuth 2.0 authentication to create and manage your secure account.
  • Gmail API Email Data: Email headers (sender, recipient, timestamp, subject, SPF/DKIM/DMARC authentication results), message body text (plain text / HTML), extracted URLs, and attachment files necessary to scan for security risks.
  • OAuth Tokens: Google OAuth 2.0 access and refresh tokens used to authenticate API requests to Google servers on your behalf.

3. How & Why We Use Your Data

We process your email data exclusively to perform cybersecurity evaluations and present threat reports to you:

  • Phishing & Spoofing Detection: Machine learning models and heuristic rules analyze email headers and text to identify deceptive senders, brand impersonation, urgent language, and credential theft attempts.
  • URL Reputation Inspection: Hyperlinks found in emails are queried against security intelligence feeds (including Google Safe Browsing and VirusTotal) to detect known malicious and phishing domains.
  • Attachment Forensic Analysis: Attachments are evaluated by our Attachment Threat Analysis Engine (ATAE) for malicious macros, executable payloads, double extensions, and anomalous MIME types.
  • Explainable Threat Insights: When requested by the user, summary explanations and threat breakdowns are generated using the Google Gemini AI API to provide transparent diagnostic guidance.

4. Google API User Data Policy & Commitments

Securamail's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements:

NO DATA SELLING: Securamail does NOT sell personal data, Google user data, or email content to any third parties.
NO PUBLIC AI TRAINING: We do NOT use Gmail data to train, fine-tune, or improve generalized or public AI/ML models.
NO ADVERTISING: We do NOT use or transfer your email data for personalized advertising, retargeting, or marketing.
LIMITED USE ONLY: All data accessed is strictly limited to providing and improving user-facing email threat analysis and security monitoring.

5. Storage, Retention & Token Security

Encrypted Token Storage: All OAuth access tokens and refresh tokens are stored in the database using AES-256 encryption at rest. Credentials are never written in plain text or logged.

Tenant Isolation: Synced email records and threat assessments are stored in a relational PostgreSQL database with strict tenant isolation, ensuring only the authenticated account owner can view their data.

Data Retention: Synced email records and reports are retained only for as long as your account remains active, enabling historical security triage and threat trends on your dashboard.

6. Third-Party Services & APIs

Securamail interacts with the following third-party services exclusively to provide security capabilities:

  • • Google OAuth 2.0 & Gmail API: For user authentication and retrieving email messages for threat inspection.
  • • Google Safe Browsing API: For checking URLs against known phishing and malicious domain lists.
  • • VirusTotal API: For reputation queries on suspicious domains, URLs, and file hashes.
  • • Google Gemini API: For generating on-demand plain-language explanations of detected security indicators.

7. User Rights: Disconnection & Data Deletion

You maintain full control over your Google account connection and stored data:

  • Disconnecting Google Account: You can disconnect your Gmail account at any time from your Securamail Settings or Profile page. Disconnecting immediately revokes our API tokens and stops all email synchronization.
  • Revoking via Google Security: You can also revoke Securamail's access at any time through your Google Account Security Permissions.
  • Requesting Data Deletion: You can delete individual email reports from the application or contact team.asteroids.2024@gmail.com to request full account and data deletion. Requests are processed promptly.
